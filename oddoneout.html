How to play

Everyone gets the same secret word — except one player, who only sees "Odd One Out."

Go around and say something about the word — specific enough to prove you know it, vague enough not to hand it to the faker.

The Odd One Out tries to blend in and figure out the word. Everyone else tries to spot them.

Then you all vote. Catch the Odd One Out and the group wins. Miss, and they got away.
