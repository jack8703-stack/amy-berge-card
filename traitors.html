How to play

A few of you are secretly Traitors. Everyone else is Faithful — nobody knows who's who.

Faithful want to find and banish every Traitor. Traitors want to survive without getting caught.

Each round: Night — the Traitors secretly murder one Faithful. Day — everyone argues, then votes to banish one person; their role is revealed.

Faithful win by banishing all the Traitors. Traitors win the moment they equal the Faithful.

Your phone tells you what to do at every step — just follow along.
