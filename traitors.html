    How to play

    A few of you are secretly Traitors. Everyone else is Faithful — and nobody knows who's who.

    Faithful want to find and banish every Traitor. Traitors want to survive without getting caught.

    Each round has two parts:

    •  Night — the Traitors secretly murder one Faithful.
    •  Day — everyone argues, then votes to banish one person. Their role is revealed.

    Faithful win by banishing all the Traitors. Traitors win the moment they equal the Faithful in number.

    The card is your host — it tells you exactly what to do at every step. Just follow along.
