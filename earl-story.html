5It keeps growing

The card never changes — the page does

Here's the part that makes it worth it: the card is printed once, but what it opens is ours to change anytime. New release drops? Update it. The card in his pocket now points to the new thing — no reprint, nothing to re-hand-out.

  • New single or video — swap the top button the day it drops.
  • A show or event — add tickets, then take it down after.
  • Save his contact — one tap drops him into your phone.
  • A holiday note — the page can say "Merry Christmas" in December and something else in July.
6Where it can go

One card, more than one thing

Down the road the first tap could show a little menu, and he (or whoever taps) picks what they want. And physically, the card could even take swappable faces — same chip, new look for a new release.
