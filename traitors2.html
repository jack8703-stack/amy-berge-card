How to play

A few of you are secretly Traitors. Everyone else is Faithful — nobody knows who's who.

Faithful want to find and banish every Traitor. Traitors want to survive without getting caught.

Each round: Night — the Traitors secretly murder one Faithful; everyone else keeps a flame lit so no one can tell who's choosing. Day — everyone argues, then votes to banish one person; their role is revealed.

At night, everyone stares at their own flame — heads down. That's the whole trick: if you look up, the table will think you're a Traitor. So keep your eyes on your phone, Faithful or not.

Shields: some nights a shield round opens. Grab one and you can't be murdered that night — but you can still be banished. Watch who lunges for one…

Faithful win by banishing all the Traitors. Traitors win the moment they equal the Faithful.
